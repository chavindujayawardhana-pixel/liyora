# Liyora Privacy Policy

Liyora is a keyboard that translates Sinhala and Singlish into English. This page explains what happens to your text.

## What Liyora reads

Liyora is a keyboard. Like any keyboard, the letters you tap go straight to the app you are typing in. Liyora reads the whole text in the text box only when you tap the red λ key. It never reads or translates password fields.

## Where your text goes

When you tap λ, the text in the text box is sent to Google Gemini, using your own Gemini API key, to get the English translation. The same happens when you tap Translate inside the Liyora app. Nothing is sent at any other time. Liyora has no server of its own.

## What Liyora stores

Nothing you type is stored. Liyora does not save, log, or share your text or the translations. The English text is kept in memory only until you tap Apply or Dismiss.

## Free and paid Gemini keys

If you use a free Gemini key, Google may use the text you send to improve its products, so avoid translating private or sensitive text. Paid keys follow Google's paid terms.

## Your API key

Your Gemini API key is stored encrypted on your phone only, and is excluded from backups. It is sent only to Google Gemini, with each translation request.

## No ads, analytics, or accounts

Liyora has no ads, no analytics, and no accounts. You can remove your key in the app, switch to another keyboard, or uninstall Liyora at any time.
